# Salapi, Design Philosophy: "Invisible Rails"

A visual movement for money that feels calm, trusted, and effortless, where
the blockchain disappears and only clarity remains.

## Manifesto

**Invisible Rails** holds that the most advanced infrastructure should be the
least visible. The Stellar ledger underneath is precise, unforgiving
engineering, yet the surface a Filipino mother touches must feel as quiet as a
passbook and as plain as a peso. Nothing on screen should hint at keys, chains,
or tokens. The craft is in the disappearance: countless hours of restraint so
the result looks inevitable, obvious, like it could never have been otherwise.

**Space and form.** Generous white canvas, a single centered column that feels
like a phone in the hand. Content sits in soft-cornered cards separated by
hairline borders, never boxes-in-boxes. Air is the primary material; every
element is given room to breathe so the eye never strains.

**Color and material.** One disciplined palette: deep ink near-black for every
word that must be read, a quiet slate for the secondary, a confident single
blue as the only voice of action, emerald reserved solely for money arriving.
The balance, the one number that matters, earns the only rich surface, a deep
indigo field. Contrast is non-negotiable: text is always dark on light, legible
at arm's length, in sunlight, by anyone.

**Scale and rhythm.** Typographic hierarchy is severe and few-noted: an
oversized tabular balance, modest section labels in small tracked uppercase,
body set comfortably large. Numerals are tabular and aligned, money must never
shimmer. Spacing follows a steady rhythm so the interface has a heartbeat.

**Composition and trust.** Every screen answers one question and offers one
clear action. Verifiability is a quiet badge, not a billboard, "Powered by
Stellar" sits small and certain at the foot, the way a bank states its charter.
The whole must read as meticulously crafted, the product of deep expertise:
painstaking alignment, master-level restraint, nothing accidental.

## Applied tokens

- Ink `#0B1220` · Slate `#5B6472` · Hairline `#E6E8EE` · Canvas `#F4F6FB` ·
  Surface `#FFFFFF`
- Action `#2563EB` · Action-deep `#1D4ED8` · Money-in `#059669` · Warn `#B45309`
- Radius: cards `1rem`, controls `0.75rem` · Shadow: single soft elevation only
- Type: Geist; balance ~40px/800 tabular; labels 11px/600 uppercase tracked;
  body 15px/500; never rely on inherited color, every text sets its own ink.
