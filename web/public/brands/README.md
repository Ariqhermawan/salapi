These SVGs are the unmodified official combination marks downloaded from the
[CoinGecko Brand Kit](https://brand.coingecko.com/resources/brand-kit), not the
sample screenshots in the attribution guide.

The `CG-GT Logos.zip` entries are:

- `coingecko.svg`: `CG-GT Logos/CG/CG-Lockup.svg`, SHA-256 `220be75f27b083773b90458592690e86fb295c1e3d4b7e35ca523ff6b18de80a`.

MarketValue links the logo to CoinGecko and keeps the required adjacent
`Data powered by` text visible, following the
[official attribution guide](https://brand.coingecko.com/resources/attribution-guide).
The attribution identifies the price source only, not a partnership or endorsement.
