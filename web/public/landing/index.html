Donation campaigns

Proof first.
Approval before payout.

Campaign terms lock at creation. The creator share is 0 to 10%, separate from a platform fee.

01

Fund

Contribute during the funding window. Funds enter the campaign's escrow.

02

Submit proof

After funding closes, the creator submits one public proof document and its hash.

03

Two approvals

Two of three configured wallets approve the same proof before review closes.

04

Release

After quorum, funds can be released to the locked creator and beneficiary wallets.

Wallet approvals and on-chain receipts do not establish real-world delivery or guarantee a campaign's claims.
